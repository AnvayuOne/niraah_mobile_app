MVP UI Map (Screen-by-screen)
Mobile — Single app, role-aware

---
A. Common

---
1. Splash / App Init
o Checks auth session, role(s), deep links, maintenance banner.

---
2. Login / OTP
o Phone/email → OTP (or password if enabled).

---
3. Role Selector (only if multi-role)
o Choose: Member / Doctor / Dietician / Trainer / Support.

---
4. Permissions & Connect Health
o Explain benefits → connect HealthKit / Health Connect.
o Skip → enable manual logging + reminders.

---
5. Notifications Permission
o Push opt-in; quiet hours.

---
B. Member (Personalized + Specialist)

---
6. Member Home (Today)
o Today checklist, quick log buttons, progress snapshot, upcoming appointment.

---
7. Plan Selection Flow
o Short Questionnaire → Plan Recommendations → Plan Compare → Select Duration → Payment.

---
8. Long Questionnaire
o Post-payment intake questions; save progress.

---
9. Prescore Loading / Pending Review
o Personalized: show immediate rules score; “refining…” state.
o Specialist: “under clinician review” state.

---
10. Progress Dashboard
• Trends (7/30d): adherence, weight/waist (if used), symptoms, sleep/activity summary.

---
11. Logging Hub
• Tabs/cards: Meal, Symptoms, Cycle, Weight/Waist, Activity, Sleep (view + manual add).

---
12. Log Meal
• Manual entry + Photo upload → async calorie estimate → user edit/confirm.

---
13. Log Symptoms / Cycle
• Symptom checklist + severity + notes; cycle start/end and flow.

---
14. Nutrition Plan
• Weekly plan + meal suggestions + swaps; preferences; shopping tips.

---
15. Workout / Yoga
• Personalized plan: pre-recorded videos by module.
• Specialist plan: cohort schedule card + join link.

---
16. Chat
• Threads: Support, Care Team; unread badges; attachments.

---
17. Appointments
• Browse experts (for add-on) / book slot; upcoming/past; reschedule/cancel.

---
18. Video Call Join
• Pre-join checks (mic/cam) → join meeting.

---
19. Medical Records
• Upload reports/labs/prescriptions; list + preview; share status.

---
20. Community
• WhatsApp group instructions + community rules; “Request to join” (ops).

---
21. Profile & Settings
• Personal info, preferences, language, consent toggles, notification settings, data export/delete request.

---
C. Doctor (in mobile)

---
22. Doctor Home
• Today: pending prescore reviews, upcoming consults, triage queue.

---
23. Member List / Assigned Patients
• Search/filter by city/language/severity.

---
24. Member Detail (Doctor View)
• Summary: prescore, trends, uploads, chat, notes.

---
25. Prescore Review
• Accept / Edit score (bounded), add flags/notes, approve visibility.

---
26. Consult Schedule
• Availability management + appointment list.

---
27. Chat (Doctor)
• Member threads; templates; attachments.

---
D. Dietician (in mobile)

---
28. Dietician Home
• Assigned members, plan updates needed, upcoming calls.

---
29. Member Nutrition Detail
• Meal logs, adherence, preferences; adjust plan.

---
30. Diet Plan Editor (MVP)
• Choose template modules + notes; publish update.

---
E. Trainer (in mobile)

---
31. Trainer Home
• Cohort sessions schedule + member list.

---
32. Cohort Session Detail
• Join session; attendance; post-session notes.

---
33. Assigned Member Detail (Trainer View)
• Activity/sleep summary; recommended module checklist.

---
F. Support (optional in mobile MVP)

---
34. Support Inbox
• Open conversations; assign/escalate.
